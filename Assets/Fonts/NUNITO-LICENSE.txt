The two TTF files in this folder were derived from the variable font Nunito[wght]
(instances at wght=600 and wght=800, reduced to Basic Latin, Latin-1, Latin Extended-A
and a few punctuation marks). They are the source for the TextMeshPro font assets in
Assets/Resources/UI/Fonts (baked by "Age of Sakura > Generate UI Art"); only those
baked atlases are part of a build.
